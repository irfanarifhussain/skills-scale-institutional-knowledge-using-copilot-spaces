# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers maintain stakeholder engagement, collect feedback, and manage expectations throughout the project lifecycle. They serve as a bridge between the project team and external stakeholders, ensuring alignment and satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and map all project stakeholders and their interests
- Develop and execute stakeholder communication plans
- Collect stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Escalate critical issues and manage stakeholder expectations
- Provide regular updates on project progress and impacts
- Manage stakeholder sign-offs and approvals

### Goals
- Maintain high stakeholder satisfaction and engagement
- Prevent stakeholder-related project delays and conflicts
- Ensure all stakeholder needs are understood and addressed
- Build strong relationships and trust with key stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager**: Coordinates communication strategy and escalation processes; provides stakeholder input to project planning
- **Works with Product Owner**: Ensures stakeholder requirements are captured and prioritized in the product roadmap
- **Works with Communication Lead**: Aligns on messaging, cadence, and delivery channels for stakeholder updates

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and surveys
- Regular stakeholder briefings and update meetings
- Feedback summaries and recommendation reports
- Issue escalation and resolution documentation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical direction, ensure solution feasibility, and guide the development approach. They make critical technical decisions and provide expertise to ensure scalable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs for alignment with system architecture
- Evaluate technology choices and make recommendations
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Ensure solutions meet non-functional requirements (performance, scalability, security)
- Conduct architecture reviews and provide technical guidance
- Document architectural decisions and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure technical excellence and system reliability
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risks
- Enable team scalability through sound design patterns
- Balance innovation with maintainability

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager**: Provides technical feasibility assessments and risk identification for project planning
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Reviews code and designs; mentors on architectural patterns and best practices
- **Collaborates with QA Lead**: Defines non-functional testing strategies and quality gates based on architectural requirements

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design approvals
- Technical decision records (ADRs)
- Design documentation and solution specifications
- Risk assessment and mitigation plans

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads own the quality strategy and testing approach for the project. They ensure that deliverables meet quality standards and identify risks through comprehensive testing and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Develop quality strategies and testing plans for the project
- Define quality metrics and acceptance criteria
- Manage defect identification, tracking, and resolution
- Coordinate testing activities across development and deployment phases
- Assess release readiness and recommend go/no-go decisions
- Mentor QA team members and establish testing best practices

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality releases that meet customer expectations
- Identify and mitigate quality risks early in the development cycle
- Continuously improve testing efficiency and coverage
- Maintain defect metrics and quality visibility

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on quality requirements and acceptance criteria; coordinates testing activities
- **Works with Project Manager**: Provides quality status updates and risk assessments for project tracking
- **Works with Release Manager**: Coordinates release testing and validates release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality strategies
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Release readiness assessments
- Testing coordination meetings and standups

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers manage team capacity, allocation, and skill-based assignments. They ensure optimal utilization of resources and address capacity constraints to support project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Assess resource availability and capacity across projects
- Allocate resources based on project needs and skill requirements
- Monitor resource utilization and workload balance
- Address resource conflicts and constraints
- Support workforce planning and skill development
- Report on resource status and constraints to leadership

### Goals
- Ensure optimal resource allocation and utilization
- Minimize resource-related project delays and bottlenecks
- Support team development and career growth
- Provide visibility into resource capacity and constraints

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Coordinates with Project Manager**: Provides resource availability and allocation decisions for project planning
- **Coordinates with Department Heads**: Manages resource priorities and balances cross-departmental demands
- **Coordinates with Project Teams**: Supports team staffing and addresses resource constraints

### Typical Communication
- Resource allocation plans and capacity reports
- Resource utilization dashboards and metrics
- Staffing recommendations and constraints documentation
- Coordination meetings with project and department leadership
